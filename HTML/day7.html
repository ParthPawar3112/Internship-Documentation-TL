Internship Day 7 Documentation

Day 7 Overview

On Day 7 of the internship, I was introduced to Vinyl Cutting, design preparation workflows, and digital fabrication concepts. I explored Autodesk Fusion and Inkscape for creating cutting-ready designs and learned how digital designs are converted into manufacturing outputs.

Introduction to Vinyl Cutting

  • Learned the fundamentals of vinyl cutting technology
  • Understood how cutting machines interpret vector designs
  • Explored practical applications of vinyl cutting
  • Learned workflow from design creation to final output

Special Session on Vinyl Cutter

Attended an interactive session conducted by Mr. Soham, where the complete workflow of a vinyl cutter was demonstrated. The session covered machine operation, design preparation, material setup, cutting workflow, and output generation.

Design Preparation Workflow

  • Prepared vector designs for cutting operations
  • Learned design optimization techniques
  • Understood scaling, alignment, and layout preparation
  • Explored cutting path concepts and workflow planning

Fusion & Inkscape Exploration

  • Used Autodesk Fusion for design exploration
  • Created vector graphics using Inkscape
  • Practiced converting ideas into fabrication-ready designs
  • Explored creative engineering design workflows

Creative Design Activity

Experimented with different shapes, layouts, patterns, and design concepts using Fusion and Inkscape. The activity focused on developing creativity while understanding practical fabrication requirements.

Design Creation Work

Documentation of the design process and preparation workflow used for vinyl and laser cutting applications.

Final Design Output

Final cutting-ready design developed through Fusion and Inkscape workflows.

Learning Outcome

Day 7 enhanced my understanding of digital fabrication, vinyl cutting workflows, design preparation, and creative engineering design. I learned how digital designs are transformed into physical outputs through modern fabrication technologies.
