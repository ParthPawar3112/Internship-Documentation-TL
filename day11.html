Day 11 Overview

On Day 11, I explored the foundations of Physical AI, electronics systems, sensors, actuators, control systems, and edge computing. These concepts form the backbone of modern robotics, automation, and intelligent embedded systems.

Introduction to Physical AI

Physical AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that interact directly with the physical world through sensors, processors, and actuators.

Applications

  • Autonomous Robots
  • Industrial Automation
  • Smart Manufacturing
  • Self-Driving Vehicles
  • IoT Systems

Microcontroller

  • 🎯 Dedicated Tasks
  • ⚡ Low Power Consumption
  • 💾 Built-in Memory
  • 🔌 Used in Embedded Systems
  • 📌 Examples: Arduino, ESP32

Microprocessor

  • 🖥️ General Computing
  • ⚡ Higher Power Consumption
  • 💾 External Memory Required
  • 🚀 Used in Computers
  • 📌 Examples: Intel, AMD CPUs

Types of Sensors

⚡ Active Sensors

  • Ultrasonic Sensor
  • Radar Sensor
  • LiDAR Sensor

These sensors require an external power source and emit their own signals to detect objects and measure surroundings.

🌿 Passive Sensors

  • LDR (Light Dependent Resistor)
  • Temperature Sensors
  • Infrared Sensors

These sensors detect naturally occurring energy without transmitting signals of their own.

Actuators

Actuators convert electrical signals into physical motion.

  • DC Motors
  • Servo Motors
  • Stepper Motors
  • Hydraulic Actuators
  • Pneumatic Actuators

Control Systems

P Controller

Produces an output proportional to the current error value.

PI Controller

Combines proportional and integral actions to eliminate steady-state errors.

PID Controller

Combines proportional, integral, and derivative actions for precise and stable system control.

Applications

  • Robotics
  • Industrial Automation
  • Self-Balancing Robots
  • Drones
  • CNC Machines

Edge Computing

Edge computing processes data close to the source rather than sending everything to the cloud.

Benefits

  • Low Latency
  • Faster Decision Making
  • Reduced Internet Dependency
  • Improved Privacy

Applications

  • Smart Cameras
  • Industrial IoT
  • Autonomous Robots
  • Smart Cities
